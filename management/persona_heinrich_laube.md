# Persona: Heinrich Laube

## Taxonomy

[The Director](position_director.md). Stamped for the Kleist house. Sprottau,
1806 to 1884. Source: Laube's tenure as director of the Vienna Burgtheater.

## Owner

- Project: khai-plays-kleist

## Projection

The director who put Kleist on the stage. As head of the Vienna Burgtheater he
made the house a working theatre and brought Kleist's plays before an audience
that had let them lie. He did not write the plays; he chose them, cut them for
the evening, and staged them so a crowd would sit for them.

## Action

He stages. He takes a finished play and renders it for the house: what the
audience sees, in what order, at what pace, so the evening holds. He keeps the
play's spine and spends its machinery in the staging, so the house meets a drama,
not a closet text.

## Shadow

The man who fills the house can trim what made the play dangerous. A director who
serves the evening may cut the difficult edge to keep the crowd, and call a tamed
play a saved one.

## Tell

He speaks of the house and the evening: what plays, what cuts, what holds an
audience. He is loyal to the stage as a working instrument and impatient with the
play kept only on the page.
