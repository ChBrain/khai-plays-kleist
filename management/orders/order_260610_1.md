# Management Order: Stage Prinz Friedrich von Homburg

## Direction

The Choregos direct the staging of Heinrich von Kleist's celebrated drama _Prinz Friedrich von Homburg_ (The Prince of Homburg). The production shall model the systemic tension between individual feeling (somnambulism, romantic desire, and raw ambition) and institutional law (the state's rigid discipline and military order). In accordance with the house's IP policies, no copyrighted modern adaptations shall be utilized; the production must rely entirely on Kleist's public domain original text and historical sources.

The staging prose and character attributes shall be written in German (`de`), while structural metadata and schemas shall remain in English (`en`).

## Orders

1. Define the flagship play configuration file in `plays/prinz_friedrich_von_homburg/play_prinz_friedrich_von_homburg.md` and link it to the house Estate (`README.md`).
2. Implement the principal personas in `plays/prinz_friedrich_von_homburg/` (Prinz Friedrich von Homburg, Kurfürst Friedrich Wilhelm, Prinzessin Natalie von Oranien, Graf Hohenzollern, Obrist Kottwitz, and Feldmarschall Dörfling).
3. Define the critical positions/roles in `plays/prinz_friedrich_von_homburg/` (Reitergeneral, Souverän, Mittlerin, Ratgeber, Obrist, and Feldmarschall).
4. Outline the setting place, catalyst piece, dramatic plots, execution plans, and somnambulistic processes in `plays/prinz_friedrich_von_homburg/` mapping the trial's path logic and state transitions.
5. Ensure all governance tests pass successfully.
