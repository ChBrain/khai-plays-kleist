# Management Order: Stage Der zerbrochne Krug

## Direction

The Choregos direct the staging of Heinrich von Kleist's comedic masterpiece _Der zerbrochne Krug_ (The Broken Jug). The production shall model the self-sabotaging mechanics of a corrupt judicial system where the presiding judge is the perpetrator under investigation. In accordance with the house's IP policies, no copyrighted modern adaptations shall be utilized; the production must rely entirely on Kleist's public domain original text and historical sources.

The staging prose and character attributes shall be written in German (`de`), while structural metadata and schemas shall remain in English (`en`).

## Orders

1. Define the flagship play configuration file in `plays/der-zerbrochne-krug/play.md` and link it to the house Estate (`README.md`).
2. Implement the principal personas in `plays/der-zerbrochne-krug/personas/` (Richter Adam, Schreiber Licht, Frau Marthe Rull, Eve, Ruprecht, and Counselor Walter).
3. Define the critical positions/roles in `plays/der-zerbrochne-krug/positions/` (Richter, Schreiber, Kläger, Zeuge).
4. Outline the court hearing processes and dramatic plots in `plays/der-zerbrochne-krug/processes/` and `plays/der-zerbrochne-krug/plots/` mapping the trial's path logic and diversion tactics.
5. Ensure all governance tests pass successfully.
