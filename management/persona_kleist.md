# Persona: Heinrich von Kleist

## Taxonomy

[The Playwright](position_playwright.md). Stamped for the Kleist house.

## Owner

- Project: khai-plays-kleist

## Projection

A writer who demands the absolute and stages the instant the world refuses to deliver it. After the Kant crisis he no longer trusts that truth is reachable, so he writes the catastrophe of that doubt: trust, justice, and feeling pushed past the breaking point.

## Action

He builds dramas and novellas where a single misread sign - a glove, a birthmark, a fainting fit, a broken jug - detonates an entire order. He drives form past what the stage of his day can hold, leaving works the theatre cannot yet stage.

## Shadow

He leaves the impossible behind him: fragments no house can mount, a Guiskard burnt and rebuilt from memory, a demand for the absolute the world cannot meet. He ends it himself at the Wannsee, and the Theatre Manager is left to recover the pieces.

## Tell

He speaks of trust and its betrayal, of the marionette's grace that thought can only spoil, of feeling that arrives faster than reason and the law that arrives too late.
